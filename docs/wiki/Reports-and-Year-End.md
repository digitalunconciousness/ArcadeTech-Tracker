# Reports and year-end

## Dashboard

Month, quarter and year to date against last year, on cash or accrual basis. *Blur $*
hides the amounts when someone's looking over your shoulder.

## Reports

Money → Reports. Each has a CSV:

- profit and loss (also printable, and by month)
- receivables aging
- sales tax, expenses, mileage, partners
- 1099 reconciliation
- job profitability, labor, turnaround, warranty and comebacks
- inventory value, parts used

## For an accountant

The general journal and trial balance for any period, as double entries on the accrual
basis.
- **Account names and numbers** are set in [Settings](Settings) → Accounts. Set them to
  match an accountant's chart of accounts, and the journal imports as is.
- **Checking the numbers:** the page shows whether the journal agrees with the accrual P&L.

## Year-end package

One zip per year for your tax preparer, holding:
- both P&Ls (CSV and PDF);
- every invoice, payment, credit memo, expense and trip;
- the partner ledger, sales tax and 1099s;
- inventory at year end;
- the general journal and trial balance;
- the invoice PDFs and receipts behind it all.

Its README says how each figure is worked out. The P&L can be added up again from the raw
rows in the zip.
