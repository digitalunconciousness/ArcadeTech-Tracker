# Parts and buying

## Parts and stock

- **Price book:** your services, labor rates and job templates (Parts → Price book).
- **Stocked or not:** a part is either **stocked** (counted, received in lots, cost of
  goods when sold) or **not stocked** (consumables like solder and flux: an expense when
  bought). The choice is fixed once the part has been ordered, received or used; to change
  it, add a new part and switch the old one off.
- **Stock:** receive, count and adjust from the part's page. A count adjustment is
  recorded with who made it, and stock is used oldest lot first.
- **Links:** saved parts pages (Parts → Links). On Android you can share a page into the
  app from the browser.

## Buying

The shopping list (Parts → Buying) collects what's running low and what jobs need.

1. *Make draft POs, one per vendor*.
2. On each PO, *Mark ordered*, add the tracking number, and attach the vendor's receipt.
3. *Receive* when it arrives.

Received parts go into stock. A part ordered for a job is set aside for that job, and the
work order leaves *Waiting on parts*.

A PO's shipping, the tax you paid on it, and any parts the shop doesn't stock show up in
[the books](The-Books) as expenses on the date it was ordered.
