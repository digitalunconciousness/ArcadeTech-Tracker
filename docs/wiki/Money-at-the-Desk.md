# Money at the desk

## Invoice ready work

Money → Invoices → *Invoice ready work* (owners).

1. Every Completed and Ready work order with unbilled lines is listed. Tick the ones to
   bill.
2. *Preview totals*, then *Issue the invoices*. Each is issued on its own: if one can't be
   (say a customer's part hasn't arrived), it says why and the rest go through.
3. Make customer links for the whole batch afterwards.

At most 50 at a time.

## Statements

Money → Statements lists everyone who owes you or has credit, with how late each balance
is.

- Tick customers for **one PDF**, a page each.
- Or make a **statement link** per customer. It shows their statement as it stands
  whenever they open it, for 30 days, and is noted in their communication log.

A statement shows open invoices, credit, what's due, and payments from the last 60 days.

## Payments

Money → Payments lists every payment, reversal and refund. Money taken before there's an
invoice is recorded from the customer's page as credit on account. To correct a payment,
see [Fixing mistakes](Fixing-Mistakes).
