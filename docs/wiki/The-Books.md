# The books

Money → Books.

- **Expenses:** *Add an expense*, with a photo of the receipt.
  - Paid with a partner's own money? Say so, and it's owed to them.
  - Purchase orders add their supplies, shipping and tax here on their own.
- **Mileage:** *Log a trip*. Each trip is valued at the IRS standard rate for its date
  (rates are under [Settings](Settings) → Books). A trip in a partner's own vehicle is owed
  to them.
- **Partners:** what each partner is owed (costs they paid, miles they drove, less
  reimbursements), plus their ledger: money put in, draws and distributions, and
  reimbursements.
- **Sales tax:** what was taxed and collected for any period; record each filing when you
  file and pay.

## Where costs land

| Cost | Where it counts |
|---|---|
| A stocked part | inventory when bought, cost of goods when it's invoiced |
| A part the shop doesn't stock | an expense when bought (supplies), never again |
| A sublet | cost of goods when it's invoiced |

Job profitability still shows every part a job used, so you can see what a job really
cost.
