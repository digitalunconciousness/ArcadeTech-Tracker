How it's booked

  • A customer's credit (a deposit, an overpayment, a credit memo not yet used) stays in Accounts receivable as a credit balance.
  • Money is booked to the account it was matched to in Money → Bank, otherwise to the account for its payment method (payments) or Checking (everything else).
  • Stocked parts are inventory when ordered and cost of goods when invoiced. Stock received without a purchase order comes in against a clearing account; stock counts, scrap and returns to vendors aren't booked here.
  • What a partner paid with their own money (or drove in their own vehicle) is owed to them until a reimbursement is recorded.
  • Voided invoices, expenses, trips and partner entries are left out.
