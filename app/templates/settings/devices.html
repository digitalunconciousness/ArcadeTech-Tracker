Devices

GATBOX units allowed to send traces and bench orders here (/api/v1, contract v1). A device signs in with a token, not a user: it sees nothing a person sees. Switch one off rather than deleting it; its traces stay.

What a device changes is made as the person it acts as and shows in the audit log as "that person via the device". Jobs lets it read each machine's open jobs and send bench orders and session tags; without it, it can still send traces.
